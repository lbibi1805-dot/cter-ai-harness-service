**1. When using the Lab PCs:**

- **Log in and get a license:** Every time you start working, you must open the **QNX Software Center** (from the desktop icon) and log in with your personal myQNX account details.
    
- **Activate:** If necessary, you can click the _Preferences_ icon on the left side to change your login details and activate the license for your current session.
    
- **Configure the SDK:** When you open QNX Momentics, you need to navigate to **Window** > **Preferences** > **QNX** to map the SDK target and host to the `C:\QNX\qnx710` folder on the lab computer.
    
- **Deactivate the license (Crucial):** After you finish your programming session, you **must** reopen the QNX Software Center and de-activate your license. This "returns" the license to the system so it is available for you to use next time.
    

**2. When using your personal PC:**

- If you are using your own PC with a personal installation, the process is much more convenient. You can simply save your login details. The system will remember them, saving you from having to manually check out and deactivate the license every time you use the software.
